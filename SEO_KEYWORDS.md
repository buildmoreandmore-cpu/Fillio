## Where to focus first

If you only do **five things**, do these. They're 80% of the conversion opportunity.

1. **`how to get bank ready for a business loan`** — own your category name. Make it the H1 of your homepage and write a 2,500-word guide.
2. **`business loan denied what to do`** — build a dedicated `/business-loan-denied` page that pitches the **Approved tier ($4,997)** and the Decline Diagnosis. This is where premium buyers live.
3. **`loan readiness assessment`** — make the Five C's Scorecard a **free tool** at `/scorecard`. Free tools rank, generate backlinks, and convert visitors to email leads.
4. **`what banks look for in a loan application`** — gate the PDF guide. Email-required download. Builds your nurture list.
5. **`personal financial statement for sba loan`** — your existing PFS generator is already strong. Build the SEO landing around it: `/personal-financial-statement-sba-loan`.

## How to outrank the competition

Your competition is **Lendio, Fundera, Nav, Bench, SmartBiz, NerdWallet, SBA.gov, and a long tail of marketplace blogs**. They have backlinks. You don't (yet). Here's how you beat them anyway.

### 1. Win the queries they don't actually answer
Fundera ranks for `business loan denied what to do` with a 2018 blog post that lists generic reasons. **They don't sell a fix.** You do. Write the page like this:
- H1: "Your Business Loan Was Denied. Here's Exactly Why — And How to Fix It in 30 Days."
- First 200 words must literally answer the question (Google's helpful-content signal).
- Embed the **free Loan Readiness Score** as a form on the page.
- End with a clear path to **Approved tier**.
- Include real example: "Founder X scored 47 in March → 81 in June → approved for $385K SBA 7(a)."

This single page beats a thin marketplace blog every time because:
- It's deeper (real product, real fix).
- It has a free tool (engagement signal).
- It has higher dwell time and lower bounce.

### 2. Build free tools that earn backlinks
Marketplaces don't give away free tools — they want you in their funnel. You can. Build:
- **Loan Readiness Score** (already in spec)
- **DSCR Calculator** (free, no email)
- **Leverage Ratio Calculator** (free, no email)
- **Collateral Coverage Calculator** (free, no email)

Free calculators get linked from accounting blogs, Reddit, university small-business clinics, and SCORE chapters. **One good calculator = 50+ backlinks over a year**, all to a page you control.

### 3. Create the document templates Google rewards
Templates rank because they have high download/engagement signals. Build a `/templates` hub:
- Personal Financial Statement template (free preview, paid full)
- Debt Schedule template
- Balance Sheet template
- P&L template
- Loan Request Letter template
- Use of Funds template

Each template page = a ranking page. Each one funnels into the BankReadyDocs product. This is the cheapest, highest-leverage SEO move you can make.

### 4. Topical authority via "Five Factors" pillar
Build a pillar page (`/five-factors-banks-score-on`) and 5 sub-pages — one for each factor:
- `/credit-profile-business-loan`
- `/cash-flow-loan-approval`
- `/financial-position-business-loan`
- `/collateral-business-loan`
- `/business-environment-loan`

Each sub-page links to the others and back to the pillar. Each one ranks for its long-tail. This is **how Bench and Nav beat marketplaces** — topic clusters, not random posts.

### 5. Local + industry pages (bottom-of-funnel rocket fuel)
Lendio doesn't have these. SBA.gov can't have these. You can.
- `/sba-loan-help-atlanta`
- `/sba-loan-help-houston`
- `/sba-loan-help-phoenix`
- `/restaurant-loan-readiness`
- `/contractor-loan-readiness`
- `/medical-practice-loan-readiness`

Each page = unique content + local/industry stats + same product CTA. Dozens of low-volume keywords stack into real traffic.

### 6. Schema markup the marketplaces ignore
Add structured data:
- `Service` schema for each tier ($297 / $2,497 / $7,997)
- `FAQPage` schema on every product page
- `HowTo` schema on the readiness assessment page
- `Review` schema once you have testimonials

This is free competitive advantage. Most blog-spam competitors don't bother.

### 7. The unfair advantage: real outcomes
Marketplace blogs can't say "this client scored 47 and got approved for $385K." You can. Every case study you publish:
- Targets a long-tail keyword (industry + loan amount + outcome)
- Beats every marketplace post for trust signals
- Gets shared by the founder you helped
- Earns real backlinks

Goal: **one case study per month**. Twelve studies = twelve ranking pages = a moat.

### 8. Backlink plays specific to this niche
- Pitch SCORE chapters (free for them, you'll get a `.org` link)
- Pitch SBA-affiliated SBDCs (`.edu` links)
- Pitch CDFIs (high-authority links to your free tools)
- Guest post on accounting/bookkeeping blogs (they want loan content, you want links)
- HARO/Qwoted: respond to every "what do banks look at" / "small business loan denial" query

### 9. Conversion-rate beats traffic
Don't get seduced by traffic numbers. The competition has more traffic but worse conversion. Your job is:
- Free tool above the fold on every relevant page
- Pricing visible in the nav
- One clear CTA per section
- Trust signals (bank-grade, SBA-aligned, real outcomes) visible in every viewport

A 3% conversion rate at 5,000 visitors/mo beats a 0.5% conversion rate at 25,000 visitors/mo. Optimize for that.

## What NOT to chase

Skip these — they look attractive but waste your effort:
- `business loans` (head term — you'll never beat Lendio/Fundera/SBA.gov)
- `best business loans 2026` (marketplace territory — affiliate game, not yours)
- `business loan calculator` (commodity term, low intent)
- `business loan rates today` (rate-aggregator territory)
- Anything that's actually a lender query — you're not a lender.
